**Engineering Manager's Leadership Approach:** The Engineering Manager coordinates team activities, assigns responsibilities, monitors workflow progress, and helps resolve blockers. Rather than solving every technical problem directly, the manager encourages developers to investigate problems independently and collaborate on solutions. This approach promotes knowledge sharing, shared accountability, and effective teamwork.

**Shared Team Responsibilities:** All team members are expected to:

- Maintain accurate GitHub issue statuses.
- Follow the established development workflow.
- Provide at least three asynchronous daily updates.
- Participate in peer reviews and verification activities.
- Follow the team's Definition of Done.
- Communicate blockers and technical dependencies.
- Contribute to collaborative decisions and process improvements.
